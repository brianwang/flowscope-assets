What to include

These four things let most problems be diagnosed from the first message:

  • Your Jira site URL (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net)
  • Whether you are on Jira Cloud, and roughly how many issues the gadget is configured to look at
  • The gadget's configuration: the JQL filter, and which statuses you picked as "started" and "done"
  • What you expected to see and what you saw instead — a screenshot helps a lot

Common questions

Why is a percentile line missing?
Percentile lines are only drawn when there are enough samples for the number to mean something. Below that threshold the line is deliberately omitted and the gadget says how many samples are needed. A 95th percentile computed from a handful of issues would just be the maximum, which is worse than showing nothing.

Why does the count not match my filter?
The gadget counts issues that have not finished yet. Issues that are done are left out on purpose. A small number can be excluded for other reasons, and the gadget always shows how many and why — it never silently counts them as zero.

An issue's age looks wrong.
The age is measured from the last time the issue changed status, so an issue that bounced back into a status is measured from when it came back. An issue that has never changed status at all has no such event to read, so its clock starts at creation — those items are counted separately and the count is shown.

Privacy

What the app reads and stores is documented in the privacy policy. In short: it reads Jira issues as you, stores a one-hour cache inside Atlassian's own storage, and sends nothing to any external service.
