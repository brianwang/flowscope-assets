Privacy Policy

LaTeX Math and Equations for Confluence

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This page describes exactly what the app does with data. It is written to match the app's source code and its Forge manifest, not to describe a general policy.

1. The short version

The app has no server, no database and no storage of its own. It sends nothing anywhere. The LaTeX text you type is stored by Confluence as part of the macro's configuration on the page — the same way any other Confluence macro stores its settings — and is typeset inside your browser.

2. What the app reads

WhatWhyWhere it goes
The LaTeX text saved in this macro's configuration It is the thing being typeset. Nowhere. It is rendered in your browser and discarded when you leave the page.
The page's theme (light or dark) To colour the formulas to match the page. Nowhere.
Your interface language To show the app's few labels in that language. Nowhere.

The app does not read Jira issues, Confluence pages, comments, attachments, user profiles or space permissions. It requests no OAuth scopes at all, which means it has no ability to read any of those things even if it wanted to.

3. What the app stores

Nothing. The app keeps no database, no Forge storage records, no logs of page content and no cache. The only persistent thing it creates is the macro configuration itself, which lives in the Confluence page and is governed by Confluence's own storage, permissions and retention — not by the publisher.

4. Network access

The app makes no outbound network requests of any kind. It declares no external egress domains in its Forge manifest. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no third-party service. The typesetting library is bundled inside the app rather than loaded from a CDN, specifically so that rendering does not require contacting anyone.

5. Data residency

The app processes everything inside your browser and inside Atlassian's own infrastructure. It defines no data egress, so there is no external location for data to reside in.

6. Children's data

The app is a workplace typesetting tool. It is not directed at children and it collects no personal data from anyone.

7. Changes

If a future version of the app starts doing anything described above differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
