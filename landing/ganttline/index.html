Gantt Timeline for Jira

Pick a JQL filter and every matching work item becomes one bar on a shared date axis, grouped into swimlanes by Epic, version, assignee, team or any field your instance has.

View on the Atlassian Marketplace

A Forge app for Jira Cloud.

What it does

  • One bar per work itemFrom its start date to its end date, with its key and summary on the left.
  • Progress inside the barFrom time spent against the original estimate.
  • A dashed today lineSo overdue reads as a distance, not just a colour.
  • Read-only by designNo dragging, no dependency lines, no rescheduling. One read-only scope, no external servers.
  • Works with any JiraThe gadget lists the date fields your instance actually has and saves your choice by field ID, so renaming a field or switching language does not break it.

What it deliberately does not do

  • It does not write to Jira
  • It does not guess datesWhere a date is missing, the app either marks the fallback it used (drawn dashed) or leaves the row out and counts it under the chart.
  • It does not invent percentagesNo percentage is derived from a status name.
