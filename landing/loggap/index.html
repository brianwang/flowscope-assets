Worklog Coverage for Jira

Of the items your team marked Done in the last N days, how many have no time logged against them - and who are they sitting with?

View on the Atlassian Marketplace

A Forge app for Jira Cloud. Free to try on a site you administer.

What it does

  • Pick a JQL filter and a window
  • One bar per assigneeGreen for completed items with time logged, amber for items without.
  • Counts, not estimatesNothing is inferred from a status name or a field label.
  • Read-onlyNo writes to Jira, no external servers.

Notes

  • Built for teams that have to reconcile timeIt answers a coverage question, not a timesheet question.
  • English and Simplified Chinese
