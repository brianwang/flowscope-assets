What to include

These four things let most problems be diagnosed from the first message:

  • Your Jira site URL (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net)
  • The gadget's configuration: the JQL filter, which field you picked as the start date and which as the end date, and the grouping field
  • Whether your Jira interface is in a language other than English
  • What you expected to see and what you saw instead — a screenshot helps a lot

Common questions

The timeline is empty, but my filter matches issues.
The chart needs both a start date and an end date on an issue before it can draw a row. An issue with a due date but no start date, and with the creation-date fallback switched off, cannot be plotted. The gadget tells you which of the two is missing and how many items were affected — it never quietly drops a row.

Some bars are drawn dashed.
A dashed bar means part of its date range came from a fallback rather than from the field you picked — usually the creation date standing in for a missing start date, or the resolution date standing in for a missing end date. The count is shown under the chart. Turning off the creation-date fallback in the gadget settings removes them.

Which field should I pick as the start date?
Whichever field your team actually fills in. Jira has no single "start date" field — it depends on your instance, on whether you use Jira Software's built-in Start date, or a custom field your organisation added. The gadget lists the date fields your instance actually has, and saves the selection by field ID, so renaming a field or switching the Jira display language does not break it.

A bar is red / orange and the end is in the past.
That is the overdue marker: the end date has passed and the item is not finished. It says nothing about why.

There are more rows than are shown.
Only the first 60 rows are drawn, taken in start-date order, and the chart says so when it happens. Narrow the JQL filter to see more of the timeline at once.

Pick a smaller date range and the chart gets truncated.
The query stops after a fixed number of pages and says so. Because rows are ordered by creation date before that cut, the items dropped are the newest ones — not the oldest, which are usually the ones you were looking for.

Privacy

What the app reads and stores is documented in the privacy policy. In short: it reads Jira issues as you, stores a one-hour cache inside Atlassian's own storage, and sends nothing to any external service.
